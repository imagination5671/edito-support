Support

Questions or problems? Email imagination5671@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description (a screenshot helps). We usually reply within 2–3 days.

What is free?

29 templates and basic text styling (bold, underline, alignment, 5 fonts, basic highlight and text colors), with up to 3 exports a day. Free users see a light watermark in the editor. Their first 2 saves have no watermark, and later saved cards include it.

What does Edito Pro include?

All 55 templates and 10 fonts, letter/line spacing, character scale, stroke and symbols, manuscript-to-cards, free layout, 12 photo effects, 4:5 ↔ 9:16 copies unlimited exports and no watermark. Renews monthly.

How do I cancel?

Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Edito. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

Restore Pro on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchases” on the Pro screen.

Where are my cards saved?

Only on your device. Use an iCloud backup or save your cards to Photos before switching devices.

Refunds

Apple handles payments; request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
